# AI Factory — Cycle Time Baselines

> **Purpose:** Establish "before" measurements for the 5 SDM workflows targeted in Phase 1.  
> **Measurement window:** 2 weeks (Days 6–20 of Phase 0)  
> **Rule:** No Phase 1 agent may be claimed as successful unless this baseline exists.  
> **Who fills this in:** SDM domain expert, tracked manually via time log during measurement window.

---

## How to Measure

For each workflow below:
1. **Start a timer** every time you perform this task.
2. **Stop the timer** when the task is complete (output handed off or sent).
3. **Record the date, duration, and notes** in the log table.
4. After 2 weeks, compute the average and fill in the Baseline Summary.
