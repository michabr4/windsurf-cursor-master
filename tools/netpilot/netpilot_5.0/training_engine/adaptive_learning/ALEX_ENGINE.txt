NetPilot 5.0 — Adaptive Learning Engine (ALE-X)

Purpose:
ALE-X dynamically adapts training content, labs, difficulty, and pacing based on learner performance, mastery, telemetry, and behavioral signals.

Core Capabilities:
- Difficulty Adjustment
- Content Recommendation
- Weakness Targeting
- Mastery Reinforcement
- Performance Prediction
- Study Plan Adaptation

Inputs:
- Learner Model
- Skill Graph
- Performance Tracking
- Telemetry Events

Outputs:
- Recommended Content
- Difficulty Level
- Study Plan Adjustments
- Mastery Updates
