NETPILOT 4.0 — PREDICTIVE TELEMETRY ENGINE (PTE)

Purpose:
The Predictive Telemetry Engine extends TIAE with forecasting and predictive analytics to anticipate failures, drift, and performance degradation before they occur.

Core Capabilities:
- Time-series forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Drift prediction
- Failure prediction
- Capacity and performance forecasting

Key Components:
- Telemetry Feature Extractor
- Forecasting Models
- Anomaly Detector
- Risk Scorer
- Prediction Store

Data Sources:
- Streaming telemetry (gNMI, model-driven)
- Historical telemetry archives
- Event logs
- Workflow outcomes

Outputs:
- Risk scores per device/link/service
- Predicted KPI trends
- Early warning alerts
- Inputs to ANE and ARE-X
