NETPILOT 4.0 — AUTONOMOUS LAB ENGINE (ALE)

Purpose:
ALE generates, adapts, and evaluates labs dynamically based on learner performance, telemetry, and platform intelligence.

Core Capabilities:
- Dynamic lab generation
- Difficulty scaling
- Scenario mutation
- Performance-aware adaptation
- Integration with ANE and PTE for “live” autonomous scenarios

Key Components:
- Lab Template Library
- Difficulty Engine
- Scenario Mutator
- Performance Analyzer
- Lab Telemetry Collector
