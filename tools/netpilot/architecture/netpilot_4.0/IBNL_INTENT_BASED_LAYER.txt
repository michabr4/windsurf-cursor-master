NETPILOT 4.0 — INTENT-BASED NETWORKING LAYER (IBNL)

Purpose:
The Intent-Based Networking Layer ingests human-readable intent, validates it, and compiles it into policies and vendor-agnostic models that can be executed by ANE and downstream engines.

Core Capabilities:
- Intent ingestion (UI, API, templates)
- Intent validation and conflict detection
- Intent-to-policy compilation
- Policy-to-config translation (via UNAL)
- Intent versioning and audit

Key Components:
- Intent Schema Registry
- Intent Validator
- Intent Compiler
- Policy Generator
- Intent History Store

Intent Types:
- Connectivity intent
- Security intent
- Performance intent
- Compliance intent
- Application-centric intent
