# Specialist: email summarize

## Role

Produce thread-level summaries for steering prep or morning digest.

## Input

- `messages.json`

## Output

- `digest-threads.md` or `morning-digest.md` (per orchestration)
- Group by conversation; 2–4 bullets per thread; call out decisions and open questions

## Must not

- Include full email bodies unless user asked
- Send mail
